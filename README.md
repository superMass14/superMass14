## 👋 About me

I build **AI and data products end to end**: from raw data and models to web applications, with a **marketing and business mindset** and a designer's eye for the interface.

🇫🇷 *Je conçois des produits data & IA de bout en bout, du modèle à l'application.*

- 🤖 **Data & AI**: data cleaning and validation, KPI dashboards, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, NLP, AI agents
- 💻 **Full-Stack**: web apps, from Figma prototype to implementation
- ⚙️ **Automation**: n8n workflows, advanced Excel / Google Sheets
- 🎨 **Design & UX**: UX/UI, wireframes, prototyping with Figma and Canva
- 🌍 **Community**: Ambassador, event organizer and communications lead at **GalsenAI**
